# Ten-minute pitch and demo runbook

## 0:00–2:00 — The problem

Introduce Amina as a fictional construction worker. She remembers what she was promised and what she received, but messages, receipts, and dates are scattered. Fairwork Pulse helps her preserve a worker-controlled record before details disappear.

## 2:00–6:00 — Live worker journey

1. Confirm the yellow **Demo** marker and state that all shown data is synthetic.
2. Open **Log a shift**, enter a construction shift, agreed pay, and amount received.
3. Attach the prepared synthetic M-Pesa image and save.
4. Open **Records** and confirm the entry survived navigation.
5. Open **Incidents**, record the related wage concern, and save.
6. Open **Haki Dossier**. Explain recorded payment gap versus estimated additional entitlement.
7. Exclude the telephone number and one attachment. Print preview to prove the exported dossier follows those choices.

## 6:00–7:00 — Safeguards and implementation

Show the PIN vault, file-integrity hash, demo-upload block, and offline status. State that AI explains information but deterministic code performs calculations.

## 7:00–8:00 — Validation and impact

Insert only completed user-test findings from `USER_TEST_PROTOCOL.md`. If testing is unfinished, say so and describe the planned measure. Never imply a completed worker or legal review without one.

## 8:00–9:00 — Sustainability

Worker use remains free. Present institution-funded training and deployment support as the business-model hypothesis to test.

## 9:00–10:00 — Closing and buffer

Return to the worker outcome: a clearer record, an explained concern, and a dossier the worker controls. Keep the final 30 seconds available for slow Wi-Fi or navigation.
