# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Delegated: Next.js-compatible React with TypeScript, Tailwind CSS, and device-local browser persistence, following the supplied architecture while keeping the first deployable MVP self-contained.

## Users

Primary users are Kenyan casual, gig, and informal workers—such as construction day workers, tea pickers, domestic workers, and delivery riders—logging work quickly on a mobile phone, often with intermittent connectivity. Secondary users are labour officers, unions, and worker advocates reviewing generated evidence and anonymized risk patterns.

## Product Purpose

Fairwork Pulse helps a worker create a contemporaneous, private record of shifts, payments, wage shortfalls, overtime, and workplace incidents, then compile those records into a structured Haki Dossier for dispute support.

## Positioning

The worker—not an employer or platform—controls the evidence vault. Statutory calculations turn ordinary shift records into an immediately useful audit trail while the core workflow remains available offline.

## Operating Context

The core session happens on a low- or mid-range phone at the end of a shift, with a target logging time under 30 seconds. Evidence may include M-Pesa screenshots, receipts, site photos, witness contacts, and brief notes. English and practical Kiswahili are required.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Log date, employer/site, times, agreed pay, amount received, and optional proof.
- Calculate wage deficit and indicative overtime using the supplied rules.
- Capture injury, maternity discrimination, unlawful termination, and wage-withholding incidents.
- Assemble an indexed Haki Dossier preview from local records.
- Show illustrative anonymized regional risk aggregates without exposing worker identity.
- Work offline-first and retain records on the current device for the MVP.
- Legal calculations and citations are informational and must not be represented as legal advice.
- Supabase synchronization and production cryptographic key management remain future integration work; the first hosted MVP demonstrates the complete user journey without requiring an account.

## Brand Commitments

The product name is Fairwork Pulse. The voice is calm, protective, direct, and respectful—never bureaucratic or alarmist. “Haki Dossier” is the name of the generated dispute package.

## Evidence on Hand

The supplied brief contains the full MVP feature specification, proposed data schema, statutory-engine example, and technical architecture. No logo, photography, testimonials, usage metrics, or verified production data were supplied and none should be fabricated.

## Product Principles

- Evidence should be faster to capture than to forget.
- The worker stays in control of identifiable data.
- Every calculation must be legible and explainable.
- Offline is the normal case, not an error state.
- Legal support language should empower without pretending to replace professional advice.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use clear language, large touch targets, strong contrast, visible focus states, and layouts that remain usable on small screens and at enlarged text sizes. English and Kiswahili must be equally reachable.
